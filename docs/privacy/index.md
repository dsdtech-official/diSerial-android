# diSerial for Android Privacy Policy

Published by DSD TECH.

**Effective date:** 2026-11-24

diSerial is a USB serial debugging tool for Android phones and tablets. You can use it without creating an account.

## USB access and local data

When you connect a compatible USB serial adapter, Android may ask you to grant diSerial access to that adapter. diSerial uses information about the connected USB device to identify it and open the serial connection. The app reads and writes serial bytes for the terminal, live visualization, and recordings you choose to make. Depending on the equipment you connect, those bytes may include personal or sensitive information.

The app stores saved recordings and their connection details, recently sent commands, shortcuts, connection presets, custom visualization profiles, and app preferences on your device. diSerial does not send this information to DSD TECH or operate a cloud sync service for it. The app does not include ads or analytics.

## Protection and Android backup

Android isolates diSerial's private storage from other apps. diSerial excludes its local database, including saved serial recordings, from Android automatic cloud backup. Android may back up eligible preferences or transfer eligible app data between devices depending on the Android version, device, and system settings.

## Exporting and sharing

You can export recordings as TXT or CSV and import or export shortcuts as JSON through the Android file provider you choose. If you choose to share a recording or open it in an email app, the selected app receives temporary permission to read the attachment. The destination service or recipient may keep or forward a copy. Review the contents and recipients before sharing.

## Retention and deletion on your device

Saved recordings remain on your device until you delete them in Logs or clear the app's storage. The app limits the number of entries in its send history. You can remove custom presets, shortcuts, and visualization profiles in the app. To remove all local app data from this device, use Android's app settings to clear storage. Manage copies you have exported, shared, or transferred separately.

## Support email and privacy requests

If you contact DSD TECH for support, we use your email address, message, and any attachments you send to handle your request. We retain this information only for as long as needed to handle your request and any related follow-up. There is no fixed retention period.

For privacy inquiries or to request deletion of information you sent to support, email [dsd_tech@outlook.com](mailto:dsd_tech@outlook.com).

## Website hosting

GitHub hosts this webpage and handles site visits under its own privacy policy. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Data safety summary

- The Android app does not automatically collect or share user data. It processes serial traffic and saved app data on your device.
- When you choose to export or share, Android passes a copy to your chosen destination. Android system backup and device transfer depend on your system settings.
- You can delete recordings in Logs or clear local app storage in Android settings.
